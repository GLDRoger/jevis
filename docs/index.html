JEVIS

How a prompt, a command, or the end of a turn travels through Jevis, from the hook to Jev to the agent.

Jevis listens at three hooks. Each time one fires, it gathers plain facts, asks Jev every question the wiki has for that moment in one call, and acts only on what clears the entry's bar. When anything goes wrong, it steps aside and the agent carries on.

      Technical data

      Where it lives. ~/code/jevis: hooks in src/hooks.mjs, the engine in src/engine.mjs, the lessons in wiki/. State in ~/.jevis, private to your user.
      Safety note. Jevis fails open. With no key, a timeout, an error, or Jev down, no note is added, nothing is refused, and no turn is held.
